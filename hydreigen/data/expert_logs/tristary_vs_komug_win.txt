Expert pilot trajectory: Tristary vs Komug win

This file stores the reusable strategic trajectory from the attached expert
battle log. The agent must generalize the lessons rather than replay this exact
game.

Opening:
- Opening hand included Basic Psychic Energy, Ultra Ball, Toxel, Secret Box,
  Zweilous, Basic Darkness Energy, and Toxtricity.
- Toxel was placed Active and Deino was benched immediately.
- Secret Box was used early, discarding Ultra Ball, Basic Darkness Energy, and
  Toxtricity because the payoff created a stronger setup line.
- Secret Box searched Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Air Balloon, Lillie's Determination,
  and Battle Cage.
- Buddy-Buddy Poffin was played immediately and found Deino plus Gastly.
- Air Balloon was attached to Gastly as a pivot.
- Psychic Energy was attached to Deino for the Hydreigon line.

Post-KO recovery:
- After Toxel was Knocked Out, Fezandipiti ex used Flip the Script immediately.
- Gastly evolved to Haunter.
- Sacred Ash recovered Toxel and Toxtricity.
- Buddy-Buddy Poffin rebuilt Bench by finding Toxel.
- Darkness Energy was attached to Haunter.
- Team Rocket's Petrel was used after board rebuilding.

Mega Gengar transition:
- Haunter evolved to Mega Gengar ex.
- Toxel evolved to Toxtricity.
- Air Balloon was attached to Toxtricity.
- Toxtricity used Sinister Surge to attach Darkness Energy to Mega Gengar ex.
- Toxtricity retreated, Mega Gengar ex became Active, and Void Gale hit for 230.
- Void Gale moved Darkness Energy to Hydreigon ex on the Bench.

Hydreigon transition:
- Psychic Energy and Darkness acceleration were used to prepare Hydreigon ex.
- Mega Gengar retreated and Hydreigon ex became Active.
- Hydreigon ex used Obsidian to KO the Active Decidueye ex and two Benched
  Pokemon, taking a four-Prize swing.

Late rebuild:
- After Hydreigon ex was Knocked Out, Fezandipiti ex drew three cards.
- Battle Cage replaced the opponent's Stadium.
- Dawn, Rare Candy, Special Red Card, Pecharunt ex, Energy Recycler, Night
  Stretcher, Toxtricity, and manual attachment were used to rebuild the final
  Hydreigon ex attack.
- Final Obsidian took the remaining Prizes.
